    How to practice

    1. Learn the characters

      Start with E, T, A and N in the Course, or choose a lesson in Settings. Tap a character to hear it.

    2. Copy the signal

      Press Play once, then type what you hear. Correct answers are checked automatically, and the next signal follows after a short pause. The cursor stays in the answer box, including while the signal plays.

    3. Use help

      Replay, listen slower, open the chart, or reveal the answer. Assisted copies earn 8 XP; a clean first copy earns 20, plus 2 XP for each quick character.

    4. Advance levels

      Mastery requires 18 quick, correct letters in your last 20 guesses, plus the requirements shown in the progress meter. Type along or enter the group after listening. Click the time badge to adjust response timing. The Course adds characters across 400 levels, then unlocks Endless radio. Choose any level in Settings.

    Enter clears a wrong answer and replays the same signal. Correct answers start the next signal automatically. Esc skips a focus level; otherwise Esc or Pause practice pauses. On a phone, tap Play once to enable sound and check device volume if needed.
